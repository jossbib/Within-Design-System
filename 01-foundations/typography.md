# Typography

The brand fonts and how to set text.

> **Status: started.** Fonts are taken from the font slide in the [brand book](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/10adt0hE1NL03DJu31Li8YJrdnTZbtgfCTYKqPR8RuIU/edit). Fallback fonts and text styles are set; rules are still to be added.

## Fonts

| Font | Used for |
|---|---|
| Proxima Nova Bold | All titles and headings |
| Gelasio | All paragraph text and small print |

Proxima Nova is available from [Adobe Fonts](https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/proxima-nova). Gelasio is free from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Gelasio), in all weights.

## Fallback font

Use these only where the brand fonts can't be loaded, for example a PowerPoint file opened on a computer without them, or an email.

| Brand font | Fallback |
|---|---|
| Proxima Nova Bold | Arial Bold |
| Gelasio | Georgia |

Georgia is the fallback for Gelasio because Gelasio was drawn to match Georgia's letter widths, so text takes up the same space and doesn't reflow.

Google Slides includes both brand fonts, so the WITHIN Slides template never needs a fallback. Don't swap fonts to make text fit.

## Text styles

Every piece of WITHIN text is one of these styles. For exact sizes and line spacing on slides, see [layout-rules.md → Text on slides](../02-presentations/layout-rules.md#text-on-slides).

| Style | Font | Colour | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display title | Proxima Nova Bold | White or Magenta | Covers and closing pages only |
| Title | Proxima Nova Bold | Black | One per page or slide |
| Eyebrow | Proxima Nova Bold | Dark grey | A short label above a title |
| Subheading | Proxima Nova Bold | Black | Sections within a page |
| Label | Proxima Nova Bold | Black, Dark grey or Magenta | Names, step numbers, chart and timeline labels |
| Lead paragraph | Gelasio Regular | Black or Dark grey | An opening sentence or two that frames the page |
| Body | Gelasio Regular | Black | Paragraphs, lists and bios |
| Quote | Gelasio Italic | Dark grey, key line in Magenta | Client quotes |
| Caption | Gelasio Regular | Dark grey | Job titles, photo credits, chart legends |
| Small print | Gelasio Regular | Dark grey | Footers, copyright, page numbers |

Titles are always bigger than subheadings, and subheadings bigger than body text. Body text is never smaller than 8 pt, except the footer.

Heading sizes step up by about a third (×1.33). Use the same ratio when setting sizes for new formats, such as web.
